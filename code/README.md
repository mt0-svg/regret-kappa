# Code

The programs behind the computations of the paper, each with its complete recorded output. The certified computations are Computation 3.13 (the constants of Theorem 3.1), Computation 4.4 (the drift inequality of Lemma 4.3) and the constants of Section 4 (Lemma 4.12 and the proof of Theorem 4.1); Lean proves all of them, and these programs redo them separately. The other programs test a proof at sample points and enter no proof. Running times are those of the recorded runs, on an AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, with PARI/GP 2.17.4 and SageMath 10.9.
