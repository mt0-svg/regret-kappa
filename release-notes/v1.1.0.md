In online linear regression in dimension one with features revealed one at a time, outcomes bounded by $B$ and a horizon $T\ge355713$ known to the learner, the minimax regret against every linear predictor lies between $B^2(3\log T-2\log\log T-15.2)$ and $B^2(3\log T+0.37)$. Chen, Qian, Rakhlin and Zhivotovskiy (COLT 2026) showed that it is of order $\log T$, which answers a question of Gaillard et al. (ALT 2019); this paper finds its constant, $3$.
