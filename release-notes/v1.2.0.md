In online linear regression in dimension one with features revealed one at a time, outcomes bounded by $B$ and a horizon $T\ge355713$ known to the learner, the minimax regret against every linear predictor lies between $B^2(3\log T-2\log\log T-15.2)$ and $B^2(3\log T+0.37)$, and a learner that knows neither $B$ nor $T$ has regret at most $M^2(3\log T+4\log\log(T+2)+12+2/\sqrt T+4/T)$ on every play, with $M$ the largest absolute outcome. Chen, Qian, Rakhlin and Zhivotovskiy (COLT 2026) showed that this regret is of order $\log T$, which answers a question of Gaillard et al. (ALT 2019); this paper finds its constant, $3$.
